# Repair route (a), v3: both fixes land, the floors survive a fresh check, and the big themes turn out to recur. 9 Oct 2026

Aviyah's decision, declared in `DECISIONS.md` before any result. Exploratory. Base: **L_cal8
settings** — match 0.70, floors solved to a 0.008 calibration target, no 0.33 minimum. **Ward
stays.** Build: `thema_L_repair`, 8,793 themes. **No rule for big themes is declared or inferred
here**; item 4 describes them and stops.

**The result that changes the picture is item 4.** Big themes look weak at the build's 0.70 match
threshold and recur strongly at 0.50: median support 0.075 against **median support-at-0.5 of 0.393**
in the 51–100 band, and 0.205 against **0.775** at 301–1000. They are not absent and they are not
noise — **they recur in a slightly different form each run**, which the 0.70 match threshold scores
as a miss. That is the reviewer's diagnostic confirmed on built themes rather than on raw clusters.

`consensus.py` and `recurrent.py` are untouched, and v0.4 still reproduces the frozen
`recurrent_dag_10770` byte for byte on all four tables.

## 1. FIX: stale merges

Applied as a post-pass on final memberships, iterated to a fixed point, **not** as an edit to
`consensus.py` — that file is load-bearing for v0.4's byte-identity proof, and changing its merge
loop in place would break the one guarantee that makes v0.4 comparable to v0.3.

```
stale pairs before 333, after 0 (asserted)
fix 1: 192 dropped (60 identical, 132 non-nested) in 2 rounds
```

The rule mirrors `consensus.classify` in what it spares: **strict containment is kept**, because a
theme strictly inside another is hierarchy and `hasse` draws the edge. Only identical sets and
non-nested overlaps at or above 0.70 are merged, and of each pair the lower-support theme goes.

It took **two rounds**, so the fixed-point loop was not decoration: dropping one theme exposed
further pairs. The build asserts zero stale pairs remain and would refuse to write otherwise.

## 2. CHAIN COLLAPSE in construction

```
fix 2, collapse share 0.90: 624 absorbed into their parent in 2 rounds; 492 kept a second support
```

The parent's membership is kept, the higher support is kept, and **both supports are recorded** in
the manifest (`repair.second_supports`), so the absorbed theme's evidence is not discarded. Edges
are re-linked by re-stacking containment over the survivors — the old edges referred to nodes that
no longer exist.

**Ordering matters and is deliberate**: collapse runs *first*, then the stale merge. Collapsing two
sibling near-copies onto their respective parents can leave those parents identical — a pair that did
not exist before the collapse. `tests/ontology/test_repair.py::test_repair_is_order_collapse_then_merge`
pins that case.

## 4. THE BIG THEMES — described, not gated

### Support at 0.70 against support at 0.50 — the finding

| band | themes | median support | **median support at J=0.5** | median gap | median coherence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 51–100 | 214 | 0.075 | **0.393** | **+0.227** | 16.2× |
| 101–300 | 28 | 0.117 | **0.515** | **+0.343** | 12.6× |
| 301–1000 | 5 | 0.205 | **0.775** | **+0.350** | 6.0× |
| 1001+ | 9 | 0.185 | **0.695** | +0.250 | **2.1×** |

A theme at support 0.075 that reaches 0.393 at the looser threshold is recurring in **39% of runs in
a form within Jaccard 0.5**, and in only 7.5% within 0.7. The individual cases are starker still:
`n06998` (51 members, viral protein maturation) has support **0.040** and support-at-0.5 of
**0.915** — present in nine runs out of ten, almost never in the same exact form. `n04158` (52,
purine deoxyribonucleotide metabolism) is 0.135 against 0.775.

**This relocates the problem.** It is not that mid-size groups fail to recur, and it is not the
floor — the floor is already at 0.025 for 10+ in this build. It is that **`THETA_MATCH` 0.70 counts a
group as absent when its membership shifts by more than 30% between subsamples**, and large groups
shift more than small ones simply because they have more members to lose. The theta grid tried
lowering it globally and could not calibrate the small strata; this says the problem is specifically
the interaction of a fixed Jaccard with theme size.

## What this means

**Both fixes do what they were declared to do, and neither is cosmetic.** 333 stale pairs are gone
and asserted gone; 624 chain near-copies are collapsed with both supports preserved; the 1001+ band
drops from 23 themes to 9; every leaf has a theme; 46% of leaves reach a mid-size home. Curated
recall is unchanged, so nothing of substance was removed.

**The floors survive the fresh check**, which was the one piece of the re-gate argument resting on a
number chosen after seeing a result. 0.00638 on seeds 4003–4005 against a 0.01 cap.

**The fixes do not fix the wide roots.** Max children 585 → 533, >30-children nodes 29 → 14. The
shape is now bimodal — 8 giant roots with hundreds of children, and 116 roots with 5–9 children that
look exactly right. Whatever produces the giants is not stale merges and not chain near-copies,
because both are now gone.

**And item 4 points somewhere specific.** The 247 big themes are all gene-coherent (every one from
51 to 1000 members above 3×, median 16×), yet most carry support below 0.2 — while recurring at
0.39–0.78 when matched at Jaccard 0.5. They recur; the 0.70 threshold does not see it, because a
large group's membership shifts by more than 30% between 80% subsamples more often than a small
group's does. The theta grid's failure was in trying one global threshold; this suggests the
threshold would need to depend on size, and — unlike the MS arm, which split on *cluster* size during
matching and changed almost nothing — the quantity that matters here is how much a group's membership
can shift while still being the same group.

**No rule is proposed on any of this.** Nothing is frozen. Aviyah decides.
