# KC2 — KC with an absolute closeness bar. 7 Oct 2026

**EXPLORATORY.** KC failed its null check because "close" was a *percentile*, so it admitted 1% of
pairs whatever the data was and could not reject a scramble. KC2 changes exactly one thing: closeness
is `cosine >= c` for a single **absolute** c, identical at every level. The fine level, the units,
the 80% join share and the overlap step are KC's, imported rather than copied.

New files only — `src/thema/ontology/kc2.py`, `scripts/kc2_sweep.py`,
`scripts/kc2_report.py`. Nothing frozen or paused is touched.

## 18:18:04Z — the answer to item 7: no, except in one cell

**Per band, is KC2 at least as good as HiDeF and A on recall and precision?**

**No.** On **recall, arm A beats KC2 in seven of the eight cells**, and not narrowly: Reactome 3–10
is 60.6% against 20.2%, Reactome 11–50 is 45.4% against 12.1%, GO 3–10 is 14.9% against 3.8%. The
one cell KC2 wins on recall is **Reactome 51–200, 11.5% against A's 7.7% and HiDeF's 7.7%** — which
is the band the whole exercise was about, so it is worth something, but it is one cell out of eight
and the margin is 26 curated sets wide.

**On precision KC2 is worse than A wherever both populate a band**, except GO 3–10 (19.8% against
11.0%). Its middle precision is the weak point: **6.2% at Reactome 51–200 against A's 22.7%**, and
**0.0% at Reactome 201–500 and across GO's middle**. So KC2's 299 mid-size themes are plentiful and
mostly do not correspond to curated biology.

**naive K still owns fine-level precision** — 58.4% and 65.7% on Reactome 3–10 and 11–50 — and still
has no middle at all.

**HiDeF owns Reactome 201–500**, 33.3% recall at every maxres, though on 3 curated sets.

### Why KC2's middle is plentiful and imprecise

The examples show it directly. Some are good: *cell cycle (III); cell cycle and transcription; cell
cycle (I); cell cycle (II); mitotic spindle elongation* (59 members), and *very long-chain fatty acid
metabolic process; fatty acid alpha-oxidation; long-chain fatty acid metabolic process; lipid
hydroxylation* (232). Others are grab-bags: *transmembrane transport (SLC cluster); obsolete
isoleucine metabolic process; L-leucine metabolic process; obsolete valine metabolic process* (95),
and *amino acid metabolism and transport; xenobiotic metabolism; metabolism in mitochondria,
peroxisome; purine nucleotide biosynthesis; mitochondrial cluster* (498).

**The mechanism is the 85% multi-parent figure.** The overlap step adds a unit to every group it is
close to 80% of, and repeating that over six levels lets a unit accumulate into many groups and lets
groups absorb each other's neighbourhoods. The result places almost everything (5 unplaced) at the
cost of boundaries: a theme of 95 or 498 members assembled this way spans several curated sets and
matches none of them at Jaccard > 0.5.

**One honest reading of the whole probe.** KC2 repairs exactly the defect it set out to repair — the
null is rejected cleanly, and at a bar that is not near the edge. It then loses on a measure KC never
got far enough to be tested on. The absolute bar was the right fix and was not the only problem.
