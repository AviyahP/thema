# Re-gate grid: the 0.33 minimum *is* what removes the middle — and opening it makes the roots worse. 8 Oct 2026

Declared 8 Oct 2026 before any result. Exploratory. No new trees. `thema_L`, `v0.3` and `v0.4`
untouched; all ten arms in new directories.

**Two headline results, and they point opposite ways.**

1. **The reviewer's reading of the manifests was right.** Removing the 0.33 minimum and gating on the
   calibration alone takes `thema_L` from **36** themes of 51–1000 members to **302**, and after
   collapsing near-copies from **27** to **196**. The middle was being removed by a fixed constant,
   not by the data.
2. **But the mechanism the re-gate was built on is refuted.** The hypothesis was that roots have
   100–227 children because intermediate nodes fail the gate and their children attach to the root.
   Opening the gate makes that **much worse**: the widest root goes from **227 children to 585**
   (cal8) or **725** (cal). Whatever produces wide roots, it is not the gate withholding
   intermediate nodes.

## §0 — where the 0.33 comes from, and why effective exceeds the floor

`DECLARED_M = 0.33` is set at `scripts/build_10770.py:78`, under the comment *"Declared rule,
unchanged from the 1,850 freeze"*. It is inherited: the master spec's §10.6 offered
`m ∈ {0.25, 0.33, 0.50}` as candidate fixed support thresholds for the 1,854-pathway build, 0.33 was
chosen at that freeze, and `DECISIONS.md` records the gate as **"max(declared m = 0.33, the per-size
floor)"**. When per-size calibration against scrambles arrived later, the old constant was kept as a
floor underneath the floors rather than replaced by them. The one line that does it is
`build_10770.py:659`:

```python
"effective": None if floor is None else max(DECLARED_M, floor),
```

So `effective > floor` wherever calibration lands below 0.33. On `thema_L` that is three of six
strata — 6-6 at 0.285, 7-9 at 0.19, and **10+ at 0.025, overridden to 0.33, a factor of 13**. Since
mid-size Ward clusters typically recur with support 0.26–0.49, the 10+ stratum is where the
override bites, and it is exactly the band the missing middle lives in.

## The grid

Three match thresholds × two floor rules, plus the three `-cal8` arms added mid-run.

| arm | match threshold | floor rule | new? |
|---|---|---|---|

| `L_cur` | 0.70 | current, 10-seed | yes |
| `L_cal` | 0.70 | calibrated, no minimum | yes |
| `L_cal8` | 0.70 | calibrated, 0.008 target | yes |
| `M5_cur` / `M5_cal` / `M5_cal8` | 0.50 | as above | yes |
| `MS_cur` / `MS_cal` / `MS_cal8` | 0.70 below 50, 0.50 at 50+ | as above | yes |

**I built the `_cur` arms on 10 seeds as matched controls**, which the brief did not ask for. Without
them a `-cal` arm differs from the published `thema_L` in *two* ways — the minimum and the seed count
— and the theta grid already showed how easily that confounds a reading. It does not here:
`L_cur` gives 2,816 themes against `thema_L`'s 2,815 and identical bands, so **10-seed calibration
under the current rule changes nothing** and `thema_L` is a valid reference.

**The `-cal8` arms were added 8 Oct 2026 before any structure result was seen, after seeing MS-cal's
held-out FDR of 0.01009** — labelled exactly as Aviyah specified. The rationale: `solve` takes the
*first* threshold whose **calibration** FDR is at or under the target, so at a target of 0.01 the
calibration estimate sits flush against the cap, and the held-out estimate — an independent draw —
exceeds it about half the time. `-cal8` solves to a 0.008 calibration target with the held-out caps
unchanged. The parameterised solver is asserted equal to `build_10770.solve` at 0.01 on every run
before being used at 0.008, so the duplicated loop is checked rather than trusted.

## The mechanism the re-gate was built on is refuted

| arm | roots | largest root sizes | **root children after collapse** | max children, any node | nodes with >30 children | multi-parent |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `L` | 40 | 2392, 2145, 2030, 1289 | 227, 193, 175, 98 | **227** | 6 | 36.9% |
| `L_cal8` | 97 | 2800, 2614, 2586, 2527 | **585, 535, 498, 466** | **585** | 29 | 65.9% |
| `L_cal` | 111 | 2835, 2662, 2634, 2571 | **725, 656, 604, 563** | **725** | 39 | 69.7% |
| `M5_cal8` | 116 | 2909, 2634, 2615, 2610 | 682, 630, 556, 551 | **682** | 75 | 72.1% |
| `MS_cal8` | 88 | 2800, 2727, 2641, 2634 | 592, 562, 540, 531 | **592** | 32 | 66.8% |

The reviewer's withdrawn explanation was replaced by this one: *"the 100–227-child roots come from the
built DAG: intermediate nodes fail the gate, and their children attach to the root."* **The re-gate
tests that directly and it does not hold.** Admitting the intermediate nodes gives the roots
**2.6× more children**, not fewer, and multi-parent nearly doubles. The new intermediate nodes do not
intercept the roots' children; they sit alongside them and add their own.

So the wide roots are not a gate artefact. On this evidence they are a property of strict containment
over a set of themes that overlap heavily — with 66% of themes having a near-duplicate, almost
everything contains almost everything smaller, and the root ends up a direct parent of hundreds.

> Among arms with FDR within the caps and no loss of small themes (3–10 within 5% of L, clarified to
> mean at least 95% of L's count), prefer the arm with the most themes of 51–1000 members, provided
> gene coherence in 51–1000 is at least 3× random and no theme has more than 30 children after

| arm | FDR in caps | 3–10 ≥95% of L | 51–1000 (collapsed) | coherence ≥3× | **≤30 children** | qualifies |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| `L` / `L_cur` / `MS_cur` | ok | 100% | 27 | ok | **FAIL — 227** | no |
| `L_cal` / `MS_cal` | **FAIL** | 140% | 235 / 240 | ok | **FAIL — 725 / 700** | no |

| **`MS_cal8`** | ok | 134% | **198** | ok | **FAIL — 592** | no |
| `M5_cur` / `M5_cal` / `M5_cal8` | ok | **61 / 87 / 85%** | 56 / 378 / 376 | ok | **FAIL** | no |

**Read literally, no arm qualifies — because the ≤30-children condition fails for the reference
too.** `thema_L` has a root with 227 children, which the reviewer's own log item 7 already recorded
as 50–227. A condition the reference fails cannot discriminate between arms, exactly as the
one-linkage coherence condition could not.

**Setting that one condition aside**, two arms pass everything else: **`L_cal8` (196 themes of
51–1000 after collapse) and `MS_cal8` (198)**. They are a statistical tie — MS's per-size threshold
adds two themes — so **`L_cal8` is the simpler of the two and the one I would put forward**, since
MS buys nothing for an extra knob.

I have not reinterpreted the rule. Both readings are stated, and the ≤30-children result is reported
as the finding it is rather than as a technicality: **it is the condition that refutes the
mechanism.**

## What this means

**The 0.33 minimum is real and it is costly.** It is a constant from the 1,850-pathway era applied
under a per-size calibration that was built to replace it, and on the 10+ stratum it gates 13× more
strictly than the data requires. Removing it multiplies the 51–1000 band sevenfold after collapsing,
gives every leaf a theme, and raises the share of leaves reaching a mid-size home from 19.5% to 43%.
The themes it admits are gene-coherent and legible.

**But it cannot simply be deleted.** Gating on the calibration alone **breaks the overall FDR cap**
(0.0101), and the 0.008-target correction that fixes that is itself a choice made after seeing a
number — declared as such, but not independently validated. And the resulting builds are far more
redundant: 66% of themes have a near-duplicate against 38.6%, and the roots gain children rather
than losing them.

**The wide roots are not the gate's fault.** That is the most useful negative result here, because it
closes off the explanation the re-gate was designed around. With two thirds of themes near-duplicates
of one another, strict containment makes almost everything a child of almost everything larger, and
a root with 585 children is what that looks like. If the goal is a navigable hierarchy, the next thing
to attack is **redundancy among themes** — near-copy collapse at build time, which the reviewer's
log already lists as a contemplated idea (NeXO-style) — rather than the merge rule or the floor.
