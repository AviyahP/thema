precisely where they bind.

**94.5% of what arm A gains and 95.8% of what arm B gains is in the 3–10 band.** The middle and
coarse bands barely move — arm B's 51–200 count is *identical* at 368. So the floors do exactly one
job, and the FDR table above says what that job is worth.

**Final-theme match between the two builds** (Jaccard ≥ 0.70):

| arm | floors → fixed-cut | fixed-cut → floors | worse direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **100.0%** | 84.3% | 84.3% |
| B | **99.9%** | 69.1% | 69.1% |

**The floors build is a strict subset of the fixed-cut build.** Every theme the calibrated build has
survives the loosened cut — 100.0% forward for A, 99.9% for B — and the asymmetry is entirely the
themes the fixed cut *adds*, which have no counterpart. So calibration is not reshaping the
ontology; it is deleting a specific set of small themes, and step 1 says those are the ones the null
also produces.

Both pass the declared caps (≤ 0.01 overall, ≤ 0.02 per stratum). Arm A's 3-seed floors are
*slightly better* on the held-out set (0.00252 vs 0.00285), which is noise rather than an

conservative.

**One real cost of three seeds, and it is arm B's:** with ten seeds arm B keeps the 5-member
stratum at a floor of 0.970; **with three seeds it drops it**. So arm B on three seeds admits no
theme below 6 members, where on ten it admits 5-member themes. That is a narrowing of the fine band
for the arm that is already weakest there, and it is a consequence of the cheaper form rather than
of the method.

**HiDeF finds zero communities on a scrambled matrix, in every band, on both seeds.** Its
persistence filter rejects structureless input outright, so **it needs no calibration at all** —
there is nothing for a floor to suppress. This is the sharpest asymmetry Test F produced: the
machinery THEMA cannot do without is machinery HiDeF does not need.

The honest caveat: a method that returns nothing on noise has demonstrated specificity, not
sensitivity, and arm B's own held-out FDR at its solved floors is 0.00189 — also very low. The
difference is that THEMA's number is *bought* with 3–10 scramble sides per build and HiDeF's is free.
