## The seven checks

**1. Roots under 50 members (12 of the 16).** Cohesion rises as size falls, 0.188 to 0.357. Several
sit just outside a larger theme: n0661 0.86 inside n0762, n0709 0.85 inside n0366, n0654 0.85,
n0793 0.83, **n0491 0.81 inside n0713**. The last is the clearest near-miss — strays 0.19 against
`STRAY` 0.10, Jaccard 13/23 = 0.57 against 0.70, so it falls in the gap between the two tolerances,
correctly by the declared rules. **None flips in any grid cell.**

**2. Level 1 is the domain tier.** n0762 (959) has 80 children reading as biological domains:
muscle contraction (61), synaptic signalling (53), PI3K/VEGFA (39), cell adhesion (36), sensory
perception (36), circulatory development (28), ion homeostasis (27), nephron development (27),

(21), bone (20), axon guidance (19), secretion (19), cAMP/PKA (19). Immunity sits under n0366 and
cell cycle under n0375, so n0762's level 1 is developmental and physiological rather than
"immunity / cell cycle / metabolism".

**3. Fragment roots — the union existed and the GATE rejected it, not consensus.**

**6. Source composition.** **3 of 16 roots exceed 80% one source** — n0654, n0274, n0574, all 100%
GO, all small. The four large roots are well mixed (GO 0.57-0.71, Reactome 0.23-0.36). **45 of 106

*Caveat carried per the plan: GO and Reactome nodes ARE pathways nesting by subsumption; THEMA's
nodes are SETS of pathways nesting by member containment. Both are containment orders; not the same
object.*

(consensus) against 0.845 (pre-consensus); 97% at >= 0.5, 85% at >= 0.7, **45% at >= 0.9**. The two
builds are indistinguishable. "Frozen" now applies to the theme set and its nesting, **not** to
exact member lists — `DECISIONS.md`, 25 Sep.
