# The gate for L1m, calibrated on scrambles as O50s does. 24-25 Oct 2026

Prompt 65, Aviyah's design. Exploratory; **nothing adopted.** L1m's 10-run bar was provisional, and
two scramble seeds showed it admitting noise below 12 pathways. This replaces it with **O50s's own
gate procedure, reused rather than reimplemented**, and the answer is not the one the replacement was
meant to give.

**The gate is correct by its own criteria and it makes the structure worse.** Both held-out caps hold
comfortably -- overall FDR **0.00528** against the 0.01 cap, every band under 0.02 -- and the floors
do exactly what was wanted at the small end: the 3-5 band falls from 10,995 themes to **2,830**, and
6-11 from 20,261 to 12,055. But the same floors let in **3.6 times more large themes**, because above
40 pathways the scrambles are empty and the rule-of-three bound puts the floor at 2 runs of 200.
Themes per pathway rise from 102.6 to **365.8**, DAG edges from 161,869 to **676,623**, roots from 617
to **4,164**, and roots of 200+ pathways from 75 to **1,263**.

So the provisional 10-run bar was doing two jobs at once: gating noise, badly, and **capping the
variant cloud at the top, accidentally and well**. The calibrated gate does the first job properly
and stops doing the second.

## What was reused, and the two things that differ

Reused from O50s, by import, not by reimplementation:

| what | where |
|---|---|
| the 13 size bands | `o50s_build.O50S_STRATA` |
| the calibration target 0.008 | `o50s_build.TARGET` |
| the rule-of-three numerator 3.0 | `o50s_build.RULE_OF_THREE` |
| one floor per band, cut ladder over every distinct support real **or** scrambled | `o50s_build.solve_stratum` |
| the held-out check | `o50s_build.confirm_with` |
| the caps 0.01 overall and 0.02 per band | `o50s_build.CAP_OVERALL`, `CAP_STRATUM` |
| applying the floors, where a band with no floor admits **nothing** | `thema.ontology.v04.gate`, `stratum_of` |
| the seed sets | `o50s_build.CALIBRATION` (3001-3010), `HELDOUT` (4001-4006) |

Two things differ, both because L1m is a different population and neither a change to the procedure.
**O50s keeps its first five strata frozen at their cal8 floors** (`FROZEN_SMALL = 5`); there is no
earlier L1m calibration to freeze, so all 13 bands are solved here. And **support is rounded to six
decimals on every side before any comparison**, which is what `Material.rows` does in O50s --
comparing raw floats across two arrays silently loses themes that should be kept, as prompt 58 found
the hard way.

Floors were solved on seeds 3001-3010 only. **The held-out seeds 4001-4006 were read exactly once**,
by the checker, and the floors were not touched afterwards.

Three things to read here.

1. **The small bands need far more recurrence than the provisional bar asked for, and less than
   O50s needs.** A 3-pathway L1m theme must be found in 97 runs of 200, not 10; but O50s's own
   3-pathway floor is 0.84, or 167 runs. L1m's small themes are *more* separable from noise than
   O50s's were, by roughly a factor of two in the required floor, in all five small bands.
2. **From 10-19 up, the floors are identical to O50s's, to six decimals.** That is not a
   coincidence to be marvelled at: 0.030151 is 6/199 and 0.0100 is 2/200, the lowest rungs of the
   support ladder. Both calibrations reach the target at the same small run-count, so both land on
   the same quantised value.
3. **From 40 pathways up, every floor is the rule-of-three bound**, because no scrambled theme is
   observed at any usable cut. The bound with ten seeds is 3/10 = 0.3 expected false themes, so a
   band passes as soon as it keeps about 38 real themes. **1280+ keeps 111 and passes easily**; that
   is why the giants all survive, and it is a statement about the scrambles being empty up there, not
   about the giants being well supported.

## What this says

1. **The calibration is sound and the result is not an improvement.** Nothing failed: both caps
   hold, the floors transfer from calibration to held-out, the solver matches an explicit loop. The
   gate is simply answering a different question from the one the structure needs.
2. **The provisional bar was a size filter in disguise.** A flat 10 runs of 200 is a *high* bar for a
   big theme -- the giants recur in 10 to 63 runs -- and a *low* one for a small theme. The
   calibrated gate inverts that: 97 runs for a 3-pathway theme, 2 runs for a 1,280-pathway one. Both
   are defensible as FDR control and they produce opposite structures.
3. **The rule-of-three bound is the whole story above 40 pathways.** With empty scrambles the floor
   can only be the lowest rung, so every large theme that exists is admitted. If large themes are to
   be gated at all, it cannot be by comparison with scrambled embeddings -- the scrambles say nothing
   there, and saying nothing is being read as consent.
4. **The small end is now genuinely clean.** 2,830 themes of 3-5 members at a floor of 97 runs, with
   a held-out FDR of 0.008, and all of them inside a larger theme. That part should be kept whatever
   happens to the rest.
5. **A size-dependent minimum support is not enough; a size cap or a redundancy rule is needed
   too.** 1,263 roots of 200+ and 365.8 themes per pathway are both worse than anything earlier in
   this line of work. The next question is not which floor but what limits the number of large
   near-copies, which prompts 62-64 have now pointed at three times.
