# Areas as one operator, applied recursively (rec3). 16 Oct 2026

**Arm W fails, and it fails in the most informative way available: the operator admits nothing at
all.** With items weighted by their pathway counts, **no support floor exists at any cut in any of
the six strata** — the best calibration FDR the solver can reach is 0.0100 to 0.0549 against a target
of 0.008. So level 1 produces zero areas, the recursion stops immediately, and the structure is the
frozen O50 unchanged.

**The ablation says the weighting is the whole cause.** Arm E — identical code, every item weight set
to 1 — solves floors in all six strata, builds 317 areas and brings the structure to 47 roots. The
two arms differ in exactly one number.

**Adopted: nothing. O50 stays as it is**, which is what the declaration says happens when W fails.

## 1. Why arm W admits nothing

`null_diagnostic.txt` in the build folder is the evidence, run with the build's own 20 calibration
scrambles. Two numbers carry it.

| | arm W (size-weighted) | arm E (equal) |
|---|---|---|
| real candidates | 6,169 | 5,685 |
| candidates per **scramble** | ~**14,400** (14,187-14,802) | ~23,500 (23,145-23,775) |
| highest support, real | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| **highest support, scrambles** | **1.000** | **0.960** |

**Best calibration FDR the solver can reach, over its own cuts** (it walks the null's support values
upward and takes the first cut reaching 0.008):

| stratum | arm W best FDR | at cut | | arm E best FDR | at cut |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 3-3 | 0.01667 | 0.975 | | **0.00385** | 0.920 |
| 4-4 | 0.02857 | 0.980 | | **0.00455** | 0.960 |
| 5-5 | 0.04167 | 0.910 | | **0.00263** | 0.830 |
| 6-6 | 0.01000 | 0.970 | | **0.00278** | 0.855 |
| 7-9 | 0.03542 | 0.925 | | **0.00132** | 0.860 |
| 10+ | 0.05486 | 0.415 | | **0.00018** | 0.320 |
| | **no floor exists, any stratum** | | | **a floor exists everywhere** | |

**The mechanism, stated plainly: under size weighting the weights carry so much of the clustering
that a position-scrambled null recurs as strongly as the real data.** Ward's merge cost is
`w_i w_j / (w_i + w_j) * ||c_i - c_j||²`. With weights running from 1 to 813 the first factor spans
nearly three orders of magnitude while the second — the squared distance between unit centroids —
spans less than one. So the merge *order* is largely a function of the weights, and the item scramble
does not touch the weights: it permutes each embedding dimension across items and leaves every weight
where it was. The scrambled sides therefore reproduce the real side's merge order closely enough to
recur at **support 1.000** — a grouping present in all 200 scrambled runs. Half of them are
indistinguishable from signal, and the recurrence test correctly refuses to call any of them real.

Note the direction of the counts: arm E's scrambles produce **more** candidates (23,500 against
14,400) and still admit a floor, because E's null candidates are *low-support* — its maximum is 0.960
and at the 10+ cut of 0.32 the averaged null count is under 1 against 374 real. W's null is smaller
but **much higher in support**. The gate is a support threshold, so what kills W is the shape of the
null's tail, not its size.

**This is a property of the declared null, and that is the honest reading.** A null that also
permuted the weight-to-item assignment would destroy the weight structure too and would very likely
admit candidates. That null was not declared, it would be a different experiment, and it would be
testing a weaker hypothesis — so no such variant was run. What the declared test says is specific:
*under this weighting, recurrence across subsamples is not evidence of position structure.*

## 6. The two criteria that are about O50, not about the areas

Two of the four criteria ccode owns are properties of a node set that includes every O50 node, and the
prompt requires exactly that set. They are worth separating from the result.

**Children ≤ 60 is failed by the frozen O50 itself.** Five O50 nodes have more than 60 direct
children, and all four giants are among them:

| node | direct children | pathways | |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `n03867` | **283** | 2,018 | giant |
| `n01845` | 277 | 2,332 | giant |
| `n02670` | 246 | 2,015 | giant |
| `n01420` | 140 | 1,412 | giant |
| `n01027` | 105 | 813 | |

So **no structure that keeps O50's nodes and its giants can pass this criterion**, whatever the areas
layer does. Arm E reduces the maximum from 283 to 242 only because the rules deleted two of the three
worst offenders. rec2fix passed the same criterion on 18 children only because it dropped the giants
out of its view — and prompt 38 asks for the giants in, on the reviewer's finding that they are wide
themes rather than grab-bags. **The criterion and the keep-the-giants decision are in direct
conflict**, and that is worth settling before any further build is judged against it.

**Roots 20-60 is likewise mostly inherited**: O50 has 188 roots, and an areas layer reduces them by
parenting. E gets to 47. W, building nothing, stays at 188.

## 8. What was proved about the method, regardless of the result

The prompt's purpose was to state the pass as one operator a paper can describe. Three claims are now
checkable rather than asserted, and each is a test in `tests/test_areas_rec3.py`:

1. **Size-weighted Ward is Ward continued from fixed clusters, exactly.** With every weight 1 the
   merge sequence, the merge counts and the heights are scipy's `ward` to **2e-15**. With integer
   weights the heights are scipy's `ward` on the **replicated point set** — `w` coincident copies of
   each centroid — to 1e-9, with the expected `sum(w) - m` zero-height merges happening first. So the
   must-link reading is literal, not a metaphor.
2. **The structure reproduces O50's own DAG.** Strict containment plus the transitive reduction over
   all 4,602 frozen nodes returns O50's `edges.tsv` — all **7,525** edges, none missing, none
   spurious.
3. **The item rule is the unit rule.** `items_of` on the level-0 structure agrees with
   `units_of` — 660 nodes plus 1 loose — derived a completely different way.

And one property was recorded before the build and held: **the frozen O50 already satisfies both
rules the structure applies** (0 twin pairs at J ≥ 0.50, no parent-child pair at or above 0.85), so
the rules over the whole set can only act on pairs involving an area. In arm W, where there are no
areas, the structure stage changed nothing: 0 collapsed, 0 merged.

Nothing in the recurrence machinery was reimplemented. The agglomeration is written here because
scipy cannot take weights; the bookkeeping goes through `linkage.run_from_merges`, which exists for
this purpose and is already proved field-for-field identical to `recurrent.tree_from_subset`.

## 9. What this says

**The primary arm fails, and the ablation localises the cause to one line of the method.** Weighting
items by their pathway counts makes the recurrence test blind: a null that scrambles positions and
keeps the weights recurs at support 1.000, so no floor can be calibrated and nothing is admitted. The
weighting was never argued, and this is the argument against it.

**Equal weighting does admit areas — and reproduces the failures already on record.** Arm E gives 317
areas, 47 roots and 91.4% summary placement, but it breaches two held-out FDR strata, puts 67.7% of
the universe in one area, keeps 242 children on a giant, and deletes 10 O50 nodes including three of
the four giants. That is prompt 34's result with the giants kept, and it fails in the same places.

**Two of the four criteria are unmeetable as written.** O50 itself has a node with 283 direct
children, so "no node with more than 60 direct children" cannot be met while the giants stay — and
the 15 Oct reviewer finding says the giants should stay. The conflict needs a decision, not another
build.

**The areas layer still does not move the external measure.** Median best Jaccard against Reactome's
29 top-level pathways is **0.273 in every pool** — O50, rec2fix with giants, W and E alike. Whatever
the areas are, they are not a rediscovery of curated top-level biology.

**Adopted: nothing. O50 stays as it is**, as declared.
