**MOTIVATION, STATED BECAUSE IT MATTERS FOR HOW THIS IS READ.** This design was written **after**
Test R's results were seen, and in direct response to one of them: **G_life, the classic lifetime,
separates real from scrambled groupings at AUROC 0.929–0.991**, better than support alone at every
size, while the leave-one-out score G_loo turned out to be strongly anti-predictive (AUROC
0.07–0.11). Arm K generalises the lifetime idea from Ward merge heights to a mutual-rank graph
ladder. **It is therefore not a pre-registered design**: the quantity it is built on was chosen
because it worked in Test R. Everything below was on file before any Arm K number existed, which is
the weaker but still meaningful guarantee available here.

A. Arms K and K-snn
1. Input: the centred, renormalised MedCPT vectors of the 10,770. Exact cosine kNN, k_max = 100, computed once.
2. Mutual rank r(i,j) = max(rank of j among the neighbours of i, rank of i among the neighbours of j), defined when both ranks are <= 100.
3. k ladder: 3,4,5,6,8,10,13,16,20,25,32,40,50,64,80,100 (the step after 100 is "128").
4. Runs s = 1..100 (seed s): keep each mutual edge with probability 0.8. Every pathway is present in every run.
   - At each k, the graph is the kept edges with r <= k.
   - Clusters are its connected components of 3 to 3,125 members. No tree is built.
5. K-snn: identical, except that at level k an edge also requires i and j to share at least ceil(k/3) of their k nearest neighbours.
6. Candidates = all clusters from all runs and k, deduplicated.
7. Support(g) = the share of runs in which some cluster at some k matches g at Jaccard >= 0.70. Containment in a larger cluster does not count.
8. Lifetime(g):
   - In run s, take the longest run of consecutive rungs where a cluster matches g at Jaccard >= 0.70, from k_a to k_b. L_s = (the rung after k_b) / k_a.
   - Lifetime(g) = the median of L_s over the runs where g is found.
9. Gate: support >= 0.33 AND lifetime >= t.
   - ONE t for all sizes: the lowest t with (scrambled kept) / (real kept) <= 0.01 overall and <= 0.02 per size stratum (3, 4, 5, 6, 7-9, 10+), on scrambles 3001-3003. They are regenerated with the build's scramble function, asserted equal, and run through this same pipeline.
   - Held-out FDR on 4001-4005.
   - t is stored; normal builds have no scramble step.
10. DAG:
    - THEMA's consensus merge (Jaccard 0.70); majority-inclusion membership; no completion step.
    - Containment edges, with multi-parent allowed.
    - Output data/ontology/v0.3x_engines/K/ and K-snn/ in the v0.3 schema, with manifest.json.
11. Report-only:
    - (a) the same build with support floors only, so we see what lifetime adds;
    - (b) a per-size t version;
    - (c) AUROC real vs scrambled per size for support and for lifetime;
    - (d) transfer of the stored t to the Reactome-only and BioLORD universes from Test R, with held-out FDR from their 4001/4002 scrambles.
12. Toy tests: two planted groups plus noise; seed reproducibility; support and lifetime against a naive implementation.
