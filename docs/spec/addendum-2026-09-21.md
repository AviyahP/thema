## The algorithm

**1. Runs.** 100 runs. Each draws a random 80% of pathways with a hashed per-run seed and clusters
the L2-normalised embeddings. Linkage is a parameter (`linkage`, default `ward`).

**2. Groupings — the half-draw cut.** Every dendrogram node with **≥ 5 members and ≤ half the
run's draw** is a grouping.

> Nodes near the top of a tree recur because they contain almost everything, not because they share
> meaning. A node over half the draw is defined by its complement, and its enrichment test is
> uninformative. **Found:** a 1,261-member "everything except immune" grouping at support 0.25,
> which the merge grew into a node of 1,640 of 1,854 pathways. This replaces the earlier full-draw
> exclusion, which it includes.

**3. Dedup.** Identical member sets across runs are stored once, remembering which runs produced
them.

**4. Eligibility.** Run *R* can judge grouping *A* if *R* drew ≥ 3 of *A*'s members. *A* is scored
only if ≥ 50 runs can judge it.

**5. Matching — two-sided.** In run *R*, walk the ancestor chains of *A*'s shared members and take
the cluster *C* with the best overlap, breaking ties toward the smaller cluster. Let
*A_R* = *A*'s members that *R* drew. *A* is **found** when both:

- **missing** = |*A_R* \ *C*| ≤ `tol × |A_R|`
- **extras** = |*C* ∩ present[origin] \ *A*| ≤ `tol × |A_R|`

> The one-sided rule looked for the smallest cluster *containing every* shared member, and returned
> not-found when none existed — so its extras test never ran at all on groupings whose members
> scatter. **Measured on the five largest immune-majority groupings: 99 of 100 eligible runs failed
> as "missing", zero as "extras".** Two-sided matching is what lets a stable core match while a
> minority of members moves.

**6. Support.** Scored per FAMILY, not per grouping — see step 8.

**7. Threshold and null.** `m` must be below 0.5: two groupings each found in a majority of runs
co-occur in some run, where they are clusters of one tree and therefore nested or disjoint, so
majority rule can only produce a tree. Overlap lives below 0.5.

The null permutes each embedding dimension independently across pathways, **re-normalises the
rows**, and runs steps 1–6 with the same seeds. **The null goes through the identical procedure,
families included** — counting scrambled *groupings* against real *families* would compare a
fragmented null with a pooled signal and understate the false-discovery rate by exactly the amount
pooling buys. For each `m`, **count** what passes on each side. **Never a maximum:** `3 × max` over
43,625 groupings returned 0.247, forcing `m = 0.742` — above the ceiling — from 35 groupings of
5–9 members.

**8. Families, before the threshold.** Build variant families across the whole pool.

- **Variants:** groupings *X* and *Y* where each differs from *X* ∩ *Y* by at most
  `max(2, 10% of |X ∩ Y|)`. One rule covering both nested pairs and the *A+s* / *A+t* case.
- **Seeds** ranked by support, then size, then bitset key. Each unclaimed grouping becomes a seed
  and claims unclaimed variants **of its own set**. No re-checking against the growing family.
- **Family support** = runs that found any member ÷ runs eligible to judge any member.
- **`m` applies to family support.**

> A theme the runs describe slightly differently splits its evidence across several groupings;
> thresholding first discards each fragment for being individually weak. Pooling raised placement
> from 65% to 92% at m=0.33. The pooling ratio is itself a discriminator: real 40,182 groupings →
> 23,271 families (1.73 each); null 43,619 → 37,225 (1.17).

**9. Membership.** Inclusion of *p* = runs where *p* is in the family's copy ÷ runs where the family
was found and *p* was drawn. **Members are kept at inclusion ≥ 0.5** — a majority of the matched
copies.

**10. Merge to a fixed point.** Recomputing membership can turn two non-variant nodes into variants.
Merge any two nodes whose final sets are variants, recompute, and repeat until none remain. Runs 3–4
rounds in practice. **Note:** the no-chaining guarantee holds at the SEEDING stage only — "repeat
until no variant pairs remain" chains by definition.

**11. DAG.** Edges are strict containment on the final member sets, immediate parents only (Hasse).
No synthetic root; the result is a forest. Pathways in no node go to `unplaced`.
