# `recurrent_dag` — current specification

*Written 21 Sep 2026; revised 22 Sep; rewritten 23 Sep for the single-merge order and the size-banded
thresholds. **Supersedes `thema-master-spec.md` §10 wherever the two differ.** Every clause is
implemented in `src/thema/ontology/recurrent.py`. Each departure from §10 exists because a
measurement forced it, and the measurement is named.*

---

## The algorithm, in order

**1. Runs.** 100 runs. Each draws a random 80% of pathways with a hashed per-run seed and clusters
the L2-normalised embeddings. Linkage is a parameter (`linkage`, default `ward`).

**2. Groupings — the half-draw cut.** Every dendrogram node with **≥ `min_size` members and ≤ half
the run's draw** is a grouping.

> Nodes near the top of a tree recur because they contain almost everything, not because they share
> meaning. A node over half the draw is defined by its complement and its enrichment test is
> uninformative. **Found:** a 1,261-member "everything except immune" grouping at support 0.25,
> which the merge grew into a node of 1,640 of 1,854 pathways. This replaces the earlier full-draw
> exclusion, which it includes.

**3. Dedup.** Identical member sets across runs are stored once, remembering which runs produced
them.

**4. Eligibility.** Run *R* can judge grouping *A* if *R* drew ≥ `min_shared` of *A*'s members. *A*
is scored only if ≥ half the runs can judge it.

**5. Count groupings — two-sided matching.** In run *R*, walk the ancestor chains of *A*'s shared
members and take the cluster *C* with the best overlap, breaking ties toward the smaller cluster.
Let *A_R* = *A*'s members that *R* drew. *A* is **found** when both:

- **missing** = |*A_R* \ *C*| ≤ `tol × |A_R|`
- **extras** = |*C* ∩ present[origin] \ *A*| ≤ `tol × |A_R|`

Matching yields each grouping's **support** and its **matched copies** — the cluster each run
matched it to.

> The one-sided rule looked for the smallest cluster *containing every* shared member and returned
> not-found when none existed, so its extras test never ran on groupings whose members scatter.
> **Measured on the five largest immune-majority groupings: 99 of 100 eligible runs failed as
> "missing", zero as "extras".** Two-sided matching is what lets a stable core match while a
> minority of members moves.

**6. Complete each grouping's members.** From its own matched copies, a grouping takes every
pathway whose inclusion — the share of matched copies holding it, counted only over runs that drew
it — is ≥ `inclusion_threshold`. A grouping therefore holds the members its origin run never drew.

> **This is new on 23 Sep and it is what removes the second merge.** Previously families were built
> on RAW grouping sets and membership was recomputed afterwards, which could turn two non-variant
> nodes into variants and forced a fixed-point merge to follow. Completing first removes the cause.
> **Cost: 4 s for ~47,000 groupings (~0.09 ms each).** **Side effect, not the reason:** 65% of real
> groupings gain members against 6% of scrambled ones — a real grouping is genuinely incomplete,
> a scrambled one has nothing to recover.

**7. Families on the COMPLETED sets.** Build variant families across the whole pool.

- **Variants:** groupings *X* and *Y* where each differs from *X* ∩ *Y* by at most
  `max(2, 10% of |X ∩ Y|)`. One rule covering nested pairs and the *A+s* / *A+t* case.
- **Seeds** ranked by grouping support, then size, then bitset key. Each unclaimed grouping becomes
  a seed and claims unclaimed variants **of its own set**. No re-checking against the growing
  family, so no chaining.
- The family's member set is the **seed's completed set**; the family exists to pool *evidence*,
  not to enlarge membership.

> A theme the runs describe slightly differently splits its evidence across several groupings, and
> thresholding first discards each fragment for being individually weak. Pooling raised placement
> from 65% to 92% at m=0.33. **The pooling ratio is itself a discriminator: real 2.89 groupings per
> family against null 1.50, a separation of 1.93 — against 1.32 for the old order.**

**8. Family support.** Runs that found any family member ÷ runs eligible to judge any member.

**9. Keep families — two criteria, banded by completed family size.**

Bands are **3–4, 5–9, 10–29, 30+**.

- **Recurrence:** family support ≥ that band's threshold `m`, with `m < 0.5`.
- **Tightness — 3–4 and 5–9 only:** the family's completed set must have centred cohesion at or
  above **c\*(s), a threshold SOLVED FOR from the declared error rate**, not a chosen percentile.
  See "Tightness threshold" below.
- **Thresholds are chosen jointly**, maximising real themes subject to an overall false rate and a
  per-band ceiling, calibrated against the **mean of five scrambles**.
- A band that cannot meet its ceiling below `m = 0.5` is **dropped**, and the build says so.

> **Why banded.** False rates differ ~100-fold across bands: at `min_size` 3 the 3–4 band produced
> 315 scrambled themes against 157 real, while the 10+ band produced **zero** at every threshold. A
> single `m` either admits the noise or discards real large themes.
>
> **Why tightness.** Any small set of mutual nearest neighbours recurs across resampling whether or
> not it means anything — stability at that scale is geometric, not biological, which is why the
> scrambled null produces small recurrent groupings at nearly the real rate. Swept from m=0.20 to
> m=0.48 the 3–4 band held **285 real against 286 scrambled**, a ratio of 1.00: recurrence carries
> no signal there, confirmed three times independently. Adding cohesion moves it to **313 / 44**,
> and **rejects zero real families at sizes 3–9** — the distributions barely overlap.
>
> **Why the mean of five.** Calibrated on one scramble the optimiser saturated its budget exactly
> (0.0500 in-sample) and returned 0.0533 on a held-out scramble. The cuts generalised; the
> optimisation had no headroom.

### Tightness threshold — solved for, not chosen

**The percentile formulation is withdrawn.** A cut at "the null's 95th percentile" admits 5% of
scrambled themes *by construction*, so the band's false rate was pinned near 0.14 by an arbitrary
constant rather than by the data. 95 was conventional, not derived, and it cannot be defended.

**Statistic — FIXED, and not revisable in response to any result.** Centred cohesion: the mean
pairwise cosine of a theme's member embeddings after subtracting the **universe mean embedding**.
Nothing else may be substituted, and no second statistic may be added if this one fails.

**Strata — size 3 and size 4 SEPARATELY.** They are not pooled.

For stratum *s* and cohesion value *c*:

- **F(s,c)** = mean over calibration scrambles of the number of scrambled themes of size *s*
  passing recurrence with cohesion ≥ *c*
- **R(s,c)** = number of real themes of size *s* passing recurrence with cohesion ≥ *c*
- **FDR(s,c)** = F(s,c) / R(s,c)

**c\*(s) = the smallest *c* with FDR(s,c) ≤ 0.02**, evaluated at **every distinct cohesion value
present in the null distribution** — no grid, no rounding.

**Recurrence is set at the grid floor, m = 0.20, for this band.** Recurrence carries no information
at 3–4 members (below), so raising it discards real and scrambled themes in equal proportion and
changes FDR not at all while losing real themes. The floor is the only defensible choice, and it is
the value every other band takes.

Calibration uses **20 scrambles** — this is a tail estimate and five is too thin. c\*(s) is then
evaluated **once** on a **fresh held-out scramble set** that took no part in deriving it.

**Failure branches, fixed in advance:**

- held-out FDR above 0.02 → **the 3–4 band is dropped from the frozen ontology**
- leave-one-out retained-theme counts spanning more than 20% → the estimate is unstable, **the band
  is dropped**

In neither case is the statistic changed, a stratum added, another rule searched for, or the target
relaxed. The build is reported without the band, with an explicit account of what is lost.

### What the 3–4 band actually rests on

**Without the tightness test the 3–4 band is 285 real / 286 scrambled.** Recurrence carries no
information at this size, and the band is decided entirely by cohesion geometry — a different
criterion from the one used above 5 members.

This holds whatever the outcome of the calibration above. Small sets of mutual nearest neighbours
recur across resampling whether or not they mean anything, so at 3–4 members stability is a
property of the metric space rather than of the biology. Every theme in this band is admitted by
geometry alone, and any claim made about the band must say so.

**10. `m` must stay below 0.5.** Two groupings each found in a majority of runs co-occur in some
run, where they are clusters of one tree and therefore nested or disjoint — so majority rule can
only produce a tree. Overlap lives below 0.5.

**11. The null** permutes each embedding dimension independently across pathways, **re-normalises
the rows**, and runs steps 1–8 with the same seeds. **It goes through the identical procedure,
families and completion included** — counting scrambled *groupings* against real *families* would
compare a fragmented null with a pooled signal and understate the false rate by exactly the amount
pooling buys. For each candidate threshold, **count** what passes on each side. **Never a maximum:**
`3 × max` over 43,625 groupings returned 0.247, forcing `m = 0.742` — above the ceiling — from 35
groupings of 5–9 members.

**12. DAG.** Edges are strict containment on the final member sets, immediate parents only (Hasse).
No synthetic root; the result is a forest. Pathways in no node go to `unplaced`.

**13. No second merge.** Families failing step 9 take no further part, and nothing edits a surviving
seed's set.

> **Variant pairs in the output are 0 by construction:** a seed claims every unclaimed variant of
> its own set, so no two seeds are variants, and deleting families cannot create one. The one way it
> could fail is the size window — `families()` walks only `2 × max(2, 10% of seed size)` around each
> seed, and completion can move a set outside it. **Measured: 0.**

## Departures from §10, each with its cause

| § | Original | Now | Forced by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10.2 | full-draw exclusion | **half-draw cut** | a 1,261-member "everything except immune" grouping |
| 10.4 | one-sided containment | **two-sided matching** | 99 of 100 eligible runs failing as "missing", zero as "extras" |
| 10.5 | support per grouping | **support per family** | evidence split across near-identical groupings |
| 10.6 | `3 × null_max` | **counts, never a maximum** | `3 × max` gave m = 0.742 from 35 tiny groupings |
| 10.7 | membership after threshold | **completion BEFORE families** | the second merge existed only to repair this ordering |
| 10.7 | one global `m` | **size-banded `m` + tightness** | false rates differing ~100-fold; 285/286 at 3–4 members |
| — | one scramble | **mean of five** | 0.0500 in-sample → 0.0533 held out |
| — | tightness at a chosen percentile | **c\*(s) solved from FDR ≤ 0.02** | 95th percentile admits 5% of the null by construction, pinning the band near 0.14 |
