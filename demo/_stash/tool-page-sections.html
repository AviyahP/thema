What you can give it

available An enrichment table you already have Output from DAVID, Metascape, g:Profiler or clusterProfiler. THEMA collapses the redundancy; it does not re-test your results.
planned A gene list and its universe Over-representation by hypergeometric test. The universe must be the genes your assay measured — we will ask for it rather than assume one.
planned A ranked gene list Every measured gene with a score, no threshold. Rank-based enrichment, with nothing discarded at a cutoff.
