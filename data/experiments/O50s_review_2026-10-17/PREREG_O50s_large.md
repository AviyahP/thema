# O50s new large themes: blind reading and intruder test. Declared 17 Oct 2026, before any reading or judging

This test applies prompt 43's declared criteria. Evaluation only; nothing feeds into construction.

**Population:** the 59 "new" O50s themes of 101-1,000 members, i.e. those with no O50 counterpart at J ≥ 0.7 (large_themes.tsv, status = new). The 28 "kept" themes are read as a reference, but they are not part of the criterion.

## R1, reading

- Each theme is read from its full member list (name and source), with no theme names shown.
- One verdict per theme:
  - **coherent:** one recognisable biological theme;
  - **linked umbrella:** 2-3 clearly related sub-themes under one recognisable idea;
  - **core with misfits:** a clear core plus 10% or more unrelated members;
  - **unlinked mixture:** unrelated blocks;
  - **incoherent.**
- **Pass:** coherent or linked umbrella in at least 90% of the 59 new themes (prompt 43).

## R2, blind intruder test (seed 20261017)

- **Insiders:** O50s nodes of 5-150 members strictly inside the theme that are not strictly inside another such node (the maximal ones). 5 are drawn at random, or all of them if there are fewer.
- **Eligibility:** themes with fewer than 4 eligible insiders are excluded and reported.
- **Intruder:** an O50s node of 5-150 members with less than 50% of its members inside the theme.
- **Items:** one per theme. Full member lists, shuffled, labelled A-F, with no names. The key is KEY_DO_NOT_SHARE_O50s_large.json and stays on the Mac.
- **Pass:** the intruder is spotted in at least 70% of eligible new themes (prompt 43). Chance is about 17%.

**Reported, not gated:** both measures on the 28 kept themes, and the results by size band.
