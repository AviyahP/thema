# Core-reading, low-overlap siblings — results (reviewer, 2026-10-11). Pre-registration: PREREG.md

240 sibling pairs (80 per J band 0.1-0.2 / 0.2-0.3 / 0.3-0.4), shared core >= 3 (median 6). Three one-sided readings
per pair (A, B, and A2 = A repeated by a different reader as a noise control); blind comparisons A-B and A-A2.

| J band | A vs B partly-or-different | A vs A2 (noise) | excess (95% CI) | "different" alone, excess |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1-0.2 | 62% (47 partly, 3 different) | 0% | +62 (+51 to +72) | +4 (0 to +9) |
| 0.2-0.3 | 49% (38, 1) | 0% | +49 (+39 to +60) | +1 |
| 0.3-0.4 | 39% (30, 1) | 1% | +38 (+27 to +48) | +1 |
| 0.4-0.7 (first pilot, no noise arm) | 23% (69 partly, 0 different; twins 11%, facets 35%) | - | - | 0 |

Declared reading: re-reading is PRESENT in all three low-overlap bands (excess >= 15 points, CI excludes 0).
The noise control is ~0%, so the A-B differences come from the different additional pathways, not reader variation.

Findings:
- Re-reading of the shared core rises steadily as overlap falls: about 23% at J 0.4-0.7, 39% at 0.3-0.4, 49% at
  0.2-0.3, 62% at 0.1-0.2. There is no sharp threshold.
- It is almost always a shift of emphasis ("partly"); a fully different reading is rare (1-4%).
- Examples: heme and Fe-S biosynthesis read as erythroid hemoglobin supply vs mitochondrial cofactor production;
  aquaglyceroporin transport read as renal water handling vs adipocyte lipolysis; glycine/glyoxylate read as an amino
  acid catabolism hub vs glycine-conjugation detoxification.
Implication: Aviyah's facet definition (the shared core is re-read) holds mostly for lower-overlap siblings; high-overlap
siblings are mostly the same core plus an extension (variants).
