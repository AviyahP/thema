# W2 — role of the shared pathways in two stories (whole-reading test, 2026-10-11)

Each item shows two overlapping clusters of human biological pathways, X and Y, with their complete member lists. Pathways present in both are marked "[shared]". Each cluster also has a "story" written by someone who saw only that one cluster.

Question: within each cluster's story, what role do the SHARED pathways play, and is it the same role in both?
- "same": the shared pathways serve the same function or theme in both stories; the differences lie only in what is added around them.
- "partly": the shared pathways serve an overlapping but clearly shifted role (a different emphasis, function, tissue or context).
- "different": the shared pathways are put to clearly different biological use in the two stories.

Use the lists to see which pathways are shared and what surrounds them; base the verdict on how the stories use the shared pathways. Judge by biological meaning, not wording.
